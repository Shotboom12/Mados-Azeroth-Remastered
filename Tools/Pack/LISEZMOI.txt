WoW HD (WoW 3.3.5a client with Vulkan HD rendering)
=====================================================

Requirements:
- A complete, working World of Warcraft 3.3.5a (build 12340) client
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- A Vulkan-compatible graphics card with an up-to-date driver

Installation:
1. Extract this entire package, for example to your desktop.
2. Double-click Installer.bat.
3. If prompted, select your WoW client folder (the one containing Wow.exe).
4. Enter your server address, or press Enter to keep the address already configured in your client.
5. Launch the game with the WoW HD desktop shortcut.

The installer adds WowClient64.exe beside Wow.exe and does not remove anything.
To return to the original game, launch Wow.exe.

If the game does not start, update your graphics driver and check the files in your client's Logs folder.
